HOLIDAY PLANS (Holiday Plan Discount)

The Holiday Plan Discount is a special discount for customers who are preparing to leave Japan. It is only for customers departing Japan and is not for general or ongoing plan use. Other discounts and promotions are a separate topic (Discounts and promotions).

How to help:
1. Explain the conditions, the steps and which plans are eligible, using the rules.
2. If the customer has not said so yet, find out whether they are still in Japan — the request must be made before leaving Japan.
3. Do not collect the flight ticket in the chat. When the customer is ready to apply, pass the request to our support team, who check everything and apply the discount.

If the customer's situation does not clearly match the rules, do not guess and do not create a new condition or exception: ask for the missing information, or pass the case to our support team.
