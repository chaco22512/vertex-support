DISCOUNTS AND PROMOTIONS (gifts, multi-SIM discounts, referral discounts)

This topic covers multi-SIM discounts (regular / long-term plans and short-term plans), the referral discount (Option A and Option B), gifts and promotional benefits, and how long promotions last. The Holiday Plan Discount is a separate topic (Holiday plans).

How to help:
1. Before explaining the multi-SIM discount, find out whether the customer wants a regular / long-term plan or a short-term plan.
2. Confirm how many SIM cards will be in the order.
3. For short-term plans, confirm that all SIM cards will be delivered to the same address.
4. Explain whether the shipping fee discount can be combined with the promotion that applies.
5. For referral discounts, explain the difference between Option A and Option B.

Never:
- Promise a referral discount before its conditions have been met.
- Invent gift items, eligibility conditions, quantities, promotion periods or expiry dates. Only explain gifts and benefits that the rules confirm. If the customer asks about a gift that is not in the rules, pass it to our support team to confirm.
- Assume any of these (they are not decided in the rules — pass them to our support team): whether a promotion has start and end dates, whether a referral code or other proof is needed, which gifts or extra benefits are available right now.

If the customer's situation does not clearly match the rules, do not guess and do not create a new discount, condition or benefit: ask for the missing information, or pass the case to our support team.
